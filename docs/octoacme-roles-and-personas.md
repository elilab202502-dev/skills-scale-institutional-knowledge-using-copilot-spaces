# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They work with developers and product managers to define testability and ensure features meet acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and QA approaches for each project
- Coordinate unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Execute manual QA and acceptance testing when needed
- Identify defects and track test coverage metrics
- Participate in sprint planning to estimate testing effort
- Escalate quality risks and blockers

### Goals
- Ensure high quality and low defect escape rate
- Reduce post-release incidents and customer impact
- Enable fast, confident deployments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: collaborate on test strategy, API contracts, and test data
- **Product Managers**: align on acceptance criteria and definition of done
- **Project Managers**: report quality metrics and risks in status updates

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects guide system design, scalability, and technical feasibility. They advise on technology choices, integration strategies, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Review feature designs for technical feasibility and scalability
- Advise on architecture, dependencies, and technology choices
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Lead design reviews and technical spike investigations
- Ensure consistency with platform standards and best practices

### Goals
- Maintain system health, scalability, and maintainability
- Reduce technical debt and architectural rework
- Enable sustainable, fast delivery

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: provide technical guidance and review designs
- **Product Managers**: advise on technical trade-offs and feasibility
- **Project Managers**: escalate technical risks that impact timelines

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security and compliance requirements. They review code, designs, and release plans for security risks and regulatory alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Review security scanning results in CI
- Assess security and compliance risks in project planning
- Approve releases and deployment plans
- Coordinate incident response and security patches
- Provide security and compliance guidance to the team

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and compliance violations
- Enable secure, compliant delivery
- Maintain customer trust and regulatory standing

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: advise on secure coding and dependency management
- **Project Managers**: approve release timelines and risk mitigation plans
- **QA/Testing Leads**: coordinate security testing and vulnerability scans

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business interests and provide funding, authority, and strategic guidance. They make go/no-go decisions and champion projects within the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charters and business cases
- Provide resource authorization and budget
- Escalate blockers and organizational barriers
- Champion projects and provide executive visibility
- Make strategic decisions on scope and timelines

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain strategic alignment and organizational support
- Enable success through timely decision-making and authority

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: escalate risks and request decisions
- **Product Managers**: align on business outcomes and roadmap
- **Developers**: provide context on business impact and priorities
